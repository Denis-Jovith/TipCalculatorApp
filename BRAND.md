# DJB — Digital Business Journey

Brand guide for Denis Jovitus Buberwa's personal/professional brand umbrella, covering
software development, ICT infrastructure, cybersecurity, blockchain, and AI agent work.

## Motto

**"Engineering tomorrow's digital journey, today."**

Alternates:
- "Your digital journey — innovated, secured, transformed."
- "From infrastructure to intelligence, built to last."

## Mission

To design, build, and secure digital systems — from full-stack software to server
infrastructure to AI-driven automation — that carry people and businesses confidently
through their own digital transformation, without cutting corners on security or quality.

## Vision

To be a trusted, end-to-end digital partner: the person or team businesses and individuals
turn to at every stage of their digital journey, from a first website to a fully secured,
automated, blockchain- and AI-ready infrastructure.

## Core values

Drawn directly from the DJB strapline — **Innovate · Secure · Transform · Excel**:

- **Innovate** — stay ahead of the curve: MERN, blockchain, AI agents, and whatever
  comes next, applied practically rather than for novelty's sake.
- **Secure** — security isn't a feature bolted on at the end; it's a default. Every system
  is built assuming it will be attacked, because eventually it will be.
- **Transform** — real technology work changes how a business or person operates, not
  just how their website looks.
- **Excel** — finish what's started, document it, and leave things better-run than they
  were found.

## Logo

- `client/public/logo/djb-mark.svg` — the square icon mark (favicon, PWA icon, navbar).
  Chrome "D"/"B" with a blue "J", on a dark rounded badge with a soft blue glow and an
  edge highlight. Legible from 512px down to a 16px browser tab.
- `client/public/logo/djb-lockup.svg` / `djb-lockup.png` — the full horizontal lockup:
  the DJB mark, a divider rule, the "DIGITAL BUSINESS JOURNEY" wordmark, and the
  Innovate · Secure · Transform · Excel strapline. Use this for documents, presentations,
  or anywhere the full brand identity (not just the app icon) is called for.

## Voice

Direct, practical, and a little informal — competent without being stiff. Explains the
*why* behind a technical decision, not just the *what*. Comfortable saying "this is
insecure, don't do it" plainly rather than hedging.
